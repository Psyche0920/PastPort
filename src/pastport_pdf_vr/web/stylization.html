Environment stylization

Upload a labelled VR export or a complete 3D tour package. The workflow detects the format, paints new source imagery and returns the corresponding animated VR or 3D tour.

1. Upload and estimate

Schema-4 labelled VR exports and native 3D tour packages with clean source panoramas. Standalone meshes and arbitrary vendor runtimes require an adapter. Maximum upload: 2 GiB.

2. Budget and generate

Blank fields use the displayed suggestions. Recorded tokens include image/context usage and are not a currency quote. One turn can exceed the threshold; it gates the next turn. The full image-turn budget is checked before any generation starts. Budgets are not raised automatically.

No automatic extra variants or repair generations. Output is a candidate for your manual review. Structural errors in the source 3D geometry are not fixed by appearance baking.
