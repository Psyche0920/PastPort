## Method and interpretation

Every source photo was inspected individually. Both complete panoramas and original-resolution detail crops were inspected; one in-memory seam reconstruction was used. No prior review, saved coordinates, database, build script or rationale was read.

Exact object identity requires matching visual form plus distinctive neighboring geometry. Continuous assemblies receive representative anchors for visible sections. Components retain separate anchors. Category resemblance and uncertain candidates are not confirmed exact appearances. Scene 2 seam fragments are not counted as separate doors.

Appearances list independently confirmed visible regions in each supplied scene. A continuous surface has representative anchors, not a pixel-by-pixel enumeration. Empty appearances mean not independently located, not rejected or proven absent.

Every row below is present irrespective of outcome. A missing panorama appearance is an abstention about visibility/identity, not a rejection of the inventory object. Source-photo notes were hypotheses, and images governed the result.
