# The Last Two Sacks

## Player promise

Two sacks seem to be missing. Yusuf, a probationary clerk, signed the ledger. If he blames Cem, the apprentice who vouched for him, he may keep his job. If he checks the records, he may have to admit his own mistake.

## The five scenes

1. **Your Name Is on the Ledger:** open the signed record and notice the missing margin note.
2. **The One Who Vouched for You:** inspect the madrasa courtyard photo, find Cem’s meeting place, and decide whether to protect him.
3. **The Two Cup Niches:** inspect the fountain and decide whether to help Halime before asking her to sign.
4. **The Kitchen Missing from the Ledger:** find the imaret’s star-patterned brickwork, then sort grain and flour into separate records.
5. **The Blank Line:** connect the ledger, loading slip, and kitchen receipt. Choose whether to correct the record, erase it, or blame the kitchen.

## Evidence boundary

The locations, architectural details, and institutional duties are sourced. Yusuf, Cem, Halime, the delivery, records, dialogue, discrepancy, and choices are fictional. Modern signs and renovations are not clues from the 1430s.

The case resolves as 12 delivered = 10 sacks of grain + 2 sacks of flour. The error is Yusuf’s classification, not a historical event or a missing waqf resource.
