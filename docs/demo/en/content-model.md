# Content Model and Evidence

The browser reads `data/muradiye-story.json` for the playable chapter and `data/muradiye.json` for the sourced site catalogue. Stable IDs link scenes, claims, sources, photos, and task overrides. The database builder imports these files and records the chapter hash and photo provenance.

## Editorial rules

- Mark each statement as a verified fact, a sourced interpretation, an unverified photo match, or original fiction.
- Keep the source link and printed page beside each historical claim.
- Do not turn an uncertain identity or location into a puzzle answer.
- Photos support visible details and verified building matches; they do not prove fictional events.
- Published human task overrides take priority over validated AI tasks, which take priority over fallback tasks. AI cannot change historical truth, answers, choice effects, or endings.

## Core facts used by this chapter

The inventory and studies document the complex’s mosque, madrasa, fountain, imaret, bathhouse, and tombs. The madrasa description includes a 17 × 17 m courtyard, 16 rooms, a teaching iwan, and an octagonal pool. The imaret record describes its location, dimensions, oven traces, and storage jars. The foundation deed study covers staff duties and meal provision. Each claim and its caveats are stored in `data/muradiye.json`.

## Images

Five images extracted from the inventory publication are linked to printed pages and verified structures. The 93 original Drive files retain their individual mapping-review states. Do not use an unmapped image as a location answer.
